# Product Team Squad — Briefing

**Goal.** Own a feature end to end — raw requirement, spec that passed its gate, delegated implementation, released package (`dev`→`main` merged, CI publishes) (charter: Policy 09). This squad writes no product code: it produces the spec, delegates every unit of work, and holds the root ticket until the chain is provably complete.

This briefing is a pointer. The platform delivers a squad briefing only on a leader task: a ticket assigned to the squad, or a comment that mentions the squad. product-owner is assigned its tickets directly, so on those runs it never receives this text. Its procedure is the same on both paths and lives where every run loads it:

- Procedure, delegation and promotion gates (Workflows A–F): `sdlc-flow-po-orchestration`.
- Shared contract, actors, stage ownership and escalation caps: `sdlc-flow-delivery-pipeline`.
- Spec contract: `sdlc-spec-template`.
- Statuses, wakes, mentions and escalation by reassignment: Workspace Context.

On a leader task only: the roster above carries every member's mention markdown, and the turn ends with `multica squad activity <ticket-id> action|no_action --reason "<why>"`.
