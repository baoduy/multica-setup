- 2026-09-29 — Policy 05 v1.13, Policy 03 v1.5, Policy 04 v1.14, Policy 07 v1.6, Policy 09 v1.16. **A child wakes its parent's owner with one handoff line on the parent; stage barriers wake nobody.** Rule (Policy 05 statement 5): a child keeps its report, `## BLOCKER` and questions on its own ticket, flips `done` or `blocked`, then posts ONE line on the parent — `<KEY> done — report on <KEY>` or `<KEY> blocked — BLOCKER on <KEY>` — with no mention of any kind on a squad-assigned parent (the platform routes an agent's plain comment there to the squad leader, in leader role), the assignee's mention on an agent-assigned parent, and nothing on a member-assigned one.
